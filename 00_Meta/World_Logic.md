# # World Logic - Minerva Project

## Purpose of This File

This file defines the highest-level rules and boundaries of the Minerva world.

It should remain short.

It does **not** explain Minerva's theories, programmes, finances, organisational structure, or individual interventions in detail. Those belong in their dedicated files.

Use:

- [[Minerva_Core_Theory]] for what Minerva believes and why.
- [[Minerva_Internal_Model]] for Minerva's internal objectives and strategy.
- [[Corporate_Structure]] and [[Funding_Model]] for organisational and financial structure.
- [[Programme_Ecosystem]] for the major programme streams.
- Individual programme files for specific recruitment methods, interventions, and participant experiences.

If a detailed file expands on something mentioned here without contradicting it, the detailed file governs that subject.

---

## Core Premise

Minerva is an international organisation devoted to increasing women's agency, leadership, professional advancement, and authority.

Its public gender-equality work is genuine. Minerva is not simply a fake NGO hiding an unrelated secret organisation.

Internally, Minerva follows a broader and more interventionist worldview. It believes professional power, domestic organisation, relationship roles, sexuality, social conditioning, and personal identity influence one another.

Minerva therefore acts in both public institutions and private relationships.

---

## Minerva Belief Is Not Automatically World Fact

Minerva sincerely believes its theories.

This does not mean those theories are objectively true within the fictional world.

When writing or interpreting material, distinguish between:

- established facts of the setting;
- Minerva's research findings;
- Minerva's interpretation of those findings;
- ideological assumptions Minerva treats as established truth.

Minerva can be perceptive, effective, biased, mistaken, or several of these at once.

---

## Gender and Role

Gender matters within the setting and within Minerva's worldview.

Minerva does not believe men and women are psychologically or biologically interchangeable.

However, Minerva rejects the assumption that sex automatically determines relationship role.

It does not believe every woman should lead or every man should provide care.

Minerva instead attempts to identify individual dispositions and what it considers suitable role alignment.

Detailed theories about leadership, caregiving, `Men`, `men`, sexual agency, social conditioning, and role alignment belong in [[Minerva_Core_Theory]].

---

## Suitability Before Intervention

Minerva does not assume everyone can be moved toward the same relationship structure.

Its programmes screen for suitability.

Potential, social conditioning, current behaviour, relationship dynamics, barriers, and strategic value may all matter.

A person's visible behaviour is not necessarily treated by Minerva as proof of their underlying disposition.

---

## Programme Separation

Private Domestic Leadership Development uses three primary recruitment channels based on participant awareness.

These channels must remain deliberately separated during recruitment and early programme stages.

They may use substantially different:

- branding;
- terminology;
- marketing;
- websites;
- participant materials;
- programme framing.

Material suitable for one channel may seriously undermine another.

The detailed channel structure belongs in [[Programme_Ecosystem]] and the relevant programme files.

## Research Culture

Minerva conducts genuine research and employs competent researchers.

Direct fabrication of evidence is not normal organisational practice.

Minerva nevertheless has strong institutional confirmation bias.

Evidence supporting its theories receives comparatively easy acceptance. Contradictory evidence tends to receive greater scrutiny for alternative explanations, classification problems, social conditioning, hidden variables, or methodological weaknesses.

This asymmetry should remain an important feature of Minerva's scientific culture.

---

## Human Agency

Minerva can use sophisticated persuasion, carefully designed environments, selective information, social influence, gradual escalation, rewards, pressure, and immersive experiences.

People nevertheless remain psychologically recognisable human beings capable of choice, resistance, disagreement, regret, and refusal.

Minerva does not rely on:

- supernatural control;
- literal mind control;
- hypnosis that removes meaningful agency;
- physical violence as a standard programme method;
- threats of physical injury.

The tension between influence and autonomy is part of the setting.

---

## Tone

The world should remain grounded.

Minerva is not a cartoon villain organisation whose members knowingly pursue evil for its own sake.

Many people inside Minerva sincerely believe they are:

- reducing inequality;
- correcting harmful social conditioning;
- helping people find roles that suit them;
- improving relationships;
- creating better conditions for female success.

Moral ambiguity should emerge from Minerva's certainty, interventionism, institutional power, confirmation bias, and willingness to push people beyond conventional boundaries.

Individual employees may be kinder, harsher, more ideological, more sexual, more contemptuous, or more cautious than Minerva's formal doctrine.

Do not mistake an individual employee's attitude for universal organisational policy.

---

## Long-Term Direction

Minerva seeks cumulative change rather than sudden revolution.

Its strategy operates through:

- individual relationships;
- professional advancement;
- female networks;
- institutional influence;
- research;
- cultural normalisation;
- increasing organisational legitimacy and soft power.

Minerva expects small changes to compound across careers, relationships, institutions, and generations.

---

## Consistency Rule

Before adding major new material, check:

1. Is this an objective fact or something Minerva believes?
2. Does it conflict with [[Minerva_Core_Theory]]?
3. Which organisation, programme, or recruitment channel is involved?
4. Is Minerva screening for suitability rather than assuming universal applicability?
5. Does meaningful human agency remain intact?
6. Is the setting still grounded and internally believable?
7. Does this information belong here, or should it live in a more specific file?

`World_Logic` defines the boundaries.

The rest of the project defines the world inside them.
