# World Logic – Minerva Project

This document defines the foundational rules and tone of the fictional world we are building. All other documents should remain consistent with this logic.

---

## Core Premise

This is a fictional world focused on female dominance, cuckolding, sissification, humiliation, and related power dynamics.

**Minerva** is an international organisation that actively encourages and facilitates these dynamics, especially within long-term relationships and marriages where the husband is the weaker or less dominant partner.

The organisation presents a polished public face of women’s empowerment, household agency, gender equality, and leadership development. This public narrative is used to obtain funding, legitimacy, and access to educated, higher socio-economic women.

The deeper reality is that Minerva promotes female-led relationships and the reshaping of weaker male partners.

---

## Public vs Internal Reality

There is a clear divide between the public face and internal culture.

**Public face**
- Language of empowerment, agency, equality, and leadership pipelines.
- Focus on helping women gain control at home so they can advance professionally.
- Presented as progressive, evidence-based, and beneficial for society.

**Internal culture**
- Harsher tone.
- Contempt for weak or “sissy” husbands is common.
- Mild to moderate misandry toward men who do not fit traditional masculine ideals.
- Stronger animosity toward traditional patriarchal structures, particularly associated with white straight male dominance.
- Some staff genuinely believe the empowerment story and think weaker men may even be happier in a submissive role.
- Other staff (especially lower-level) are motivated by power, status, or personal enjoyment of the dynamics.

---

## Target Group

Primary focus:
- Married women or women in long-term relationships
- With weaker, passive, or less dominant husbands
- Higher socio-economic status
- More educated

Programme types currently envisioned:
1. **Main programme** – Women who do not yet realise they want a female-led dynamic. They are introduced and gradually guided.
2. **Aware programme** – Women who already want a female-led relationship and need practical help reshaping their partner.
3. Weak husbands reaching out may be integrated into the main programme rather than forming a fully separate track.

---

## Methods

Allowed methods include:
- Education and reframing of norms
- Social pressure
- Psychological pressure
- Guilt
- Gaslighting
- Gradual escalation and guided experiences
- Clinical, structured programmes
- Threats of exposure or social consequences (including sharing humiliating pictures or information)

Not allowed:
- Physical force or violence
- Threats of physical injury
- Hypnosis or techniques that fully remove agency

The process often begins with mild reluctance, uncertainty, or shame about breaking cultural norms. Explicit sexual outcomes (including a husband being sexually used by his wife’s new partner) can appear later, but they grow from the relationship dynamic rather than from force.

---

## Attitude Toward Men

- “Real” masculine men are generally respected as real men.
- Weak, passive, or non-traditional men are viewed with contempt or mild disdain, especially when they still try to claim traditional male authority.
- Because a large portion of the male population does not strongly embody the traditional masculine archetype, Minerva’s methods and worldview affect a significant part of the male population.

Race play / BNWO elements exist but are secondary and only used in specific contexts.

---

## Tone and Style Guidelines

- Early and public-facing material should feel relatively realistic and grounded.
- Manuals and internal documents should stay clinical in tone.
- The world should never become cartoonishly dark or sadistic.
- Slight reluctance, shame, or social discomfort is acceptable and expected.
- Explicit content is allowed later in the development of relationships and programmes, but it should feel like a natural (if extreme) outcome of the power shift rather than pure cruelty.

---

## Geographic and Cultural Scope

- International, English-language primary.
- Main focus on Europe and North America (regions with relatively high formal gender equality).
- Other locations (including a possible Caribbean island base or retreat) can be introduced as needed.

---

## Long-term Direction

Minerva aims for a slow cultural shift rather than a sudden revolution.  

By elevating women into positions of influence and creating networks of women who have gone through its programmes, the organisation increases its own soft power. The goal is a broader normalisation of female-led dynamics and the corresponding reshaping of many male partners, not a single apocalyptic end-state.

---

## Working Rule

When creating new material, always ask:

- Is this consistent with the public face, the internal culture, or both?
- Does it stay within the allowed methods (no physical force or violence)?
- Does it match the target demographic and tone?

This document is the reference point for consistency.
