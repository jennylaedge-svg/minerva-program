## Session Context

Claire has expressed discomfort with the programme's suggestion that she should give greater weight to her own ambitions and preferences.

She interprets increased support from Daniel as transferring unwanted responsibilities onto him. Claire receives little intrinsic satisfaction from domestic work herself and assumes Daniel would experience increased domestic responsibility in approximately the same way.

During earlier Day 2 sessions, participants have been introduced to the idea that many relationship roles are inherited rather than consciously chosen, and that conventional expectations can restrict both partners.

Claire has begun accepting that women are often taught to accommodate and that men are often taught to prove themselves through career, status, leadership, and independence. She does not yet recognise how strongly these expectations may influence her assumptions about Daniel.

Minerva's existing assessment indicates that Daniel may have a substantially stronger caregiving/support orientation than Claire currently recognises. This has not been presented to Claire as a conclusion.

The immediate objective is not to convince Claire that Daniel should assume a particular role. It is to challenge her assumption that increased support would necessarily be harmful or unwanted, and to establish that social expectations may make it difficult for Daniel to recognise or admit preferences that conflict with conventional masculinity.
