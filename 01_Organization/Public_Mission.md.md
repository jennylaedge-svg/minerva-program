# Public Mission – Minerva

Minerva is an international organisation dedicated to advancing gender equality, women’s leadership, and inclusive social progress.

We work with governments, businesses, academic institutions, and civil society partners to create the conditions in which women can fully participate in economic, political, and public life. Our programmes support women in developing the skills, confidence, networks, and structural opportunities needed to take on senior leadership roles across sectors.

Minerva’s work is grounded in the belief that lasting equality requires change at multiple levels: individual, organisational, and societal. We focus on evidence-based approaches that strengthen women’s agency, reduce barriers to advancement, and promote more equitable norms in both public and private spheres.

Our key areas of focus include:

- Women’s leadership development and career progression
- Gender equality in the workplace and in public institutions
- Supporting inclusive cultures that enable women from diverse backgrounds to succeed
- Research, advocacy, and partnership-building to drive systemic change

Minerva collaborates with a wide range of stakeholders committed to a more equal and representative future. We believe that when women are able to lead without structural or cultural barriers, societies and organisations benefit as a whole.
